Points & Eligibility

Eligibility Formula
TOTAL = RANKING + BUFFER
📈
Ranking Points

Your true skill rating. Rises when you win, drops when you lose.

⚡
Buffer Points

A temporary starting boost so new players can find matches instantly.

🎯
20-Match Calibration

Your buffer decays by 5% each match. After 20 matches, it becomes zero.
